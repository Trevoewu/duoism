This note demonstrates standard Markdown elements styled for comfortable reading in both Sky Light and Klein Blue Dark modes.

## Lists & Quotes

- Clean monospace rhythm with `IBM Plex Mono` and `IBM Plex Sans SC`
- Automatic hyphenation and balanced headings
- Accessible focus rings and subtle underlines

> Simplicity is about subtracting the obvious and adding the meaningful.
