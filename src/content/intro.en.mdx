Hello and welcome to **Duoism** — a minimal Astro theme for personal notes and research essays, evolved from [Twoism](https://twoism.pages.dev).

It pairs a sky-tinted light palette with Klein-blue dark mode, and ships with first-class bilingual switching (`中 / EN`), automatic build-time Atkinson image dithering, wide-screen TOC, MathJax equations, and interactive SVG charts.
